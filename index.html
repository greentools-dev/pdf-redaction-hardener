How to Permanently Redact Documents

Standard black rectangles in Acrobat or Word leave the underlying text layer intact. Here is how true pixel redaction works:

1

Drop Your PDF or Image

Drop your document into the box. PDF.js immediately renders the pages onto a high-resolution local canvas.

2

Click & Drag Blackout Boxes

Draw rectangles over bank balances, credit card numbers, Social Security numbers, or names. You can redact multiple sections across any page.

3

Export Flattened PDF

Click Export Flattened PDF. The tool burns your black boxes into the page pixels and packages them into a clean PDF. No recipient can highlight, copy, or unmask the underlying text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can traditional PDF redactions be reversed?

Standard PDF annotators only draw a visual rectangle in an annotation layer. If the author forgets to execute a destructive "Sanitize Document" step, the underlying vector fonts and searchable text remain present under the black box.

How does this tool guarantee irreversible redaction?

We render the document onto an HTML5 canvas bitmap, paint pure black pixels directly over the coordinates, and export a fresh PDF composed strictly of raster page images. The vector text layer is destroyed completely.

Can I redact multi-page PDFs?

Yes. You can navigate between pages, draw redactions on any page, and export the complete multi-page document into a single flattened PDF.

Does this tool work completely offline?

Yes. All rendering and generation scripts are self-contained and run fully offline without an internet connection.
